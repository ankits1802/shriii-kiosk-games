# Consent script: think-aloud audio (`consent.research_audio`)

**Version:** 0.1 DRAFT, 2026-10-21. English source text for recording; not approved (see the [consent index](README.md)).

**Kind:** `research_audio`. **Where:** supervised sites only, Stage 1 and 1b think-aloud (CRT-08). Not
available at open public kiosks.
**Delivered by:** researcher reads aloud; the participant taps on the researcher device.
**Decision:** tap yes or no, **default no**. A participant who says no can still take part in the
think-aloud with written observer notes only.
**Records:** consent artefact `kind: research_audio`.
**Deletion:** audio deleted within 30 days after coding is complete (and at most 60 days after recording).

## Script

### Segment 1 (`consent.research_audio.purpose`)

> Today we are testing whether the games are easy to understand. While you play, please say out loud what
> you are thinking: what you see, what you expect, anything that is confusing. There are no wrong answers;
> if something is confusing, that is our mistake, not yours.

### Segment 2 (`consent.research_audio.recording`)

> With your permission, we would like to record your voice while you talk, so we do not miss anything. Only
> the research team listens to it, to make notes. We delete the recording within a month after making the
> notes. The notes do not include your name.

### Segment 3 (`consent.research_audio.voluntary`)

> You can say no to the recording and still help us; we will just write notes. You can ask us to stop
> recording, or to delete it, at any time. Questions or complaints: the help desk, [phone number], or the
> Data Protection Board of India.

### Decision prompt (`consent.research_audio.decision`)

> May we record your voice while you play and talk? Touch the tick for yes, or the cross for no.

## Notes

- The participant code links the observer notes and the game events (manual linkage on both devices).
- The summary shown in think-aloud sessions uses demo profiles only (`report_mode=demo`), never the
  participant's own results.
- **[IEC REVIEW]** whether quotes from recordings may be used, without names, in study documents (draft
  says no beyond internal notes).
