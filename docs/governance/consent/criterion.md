# Consent script: criterion (`consent.criterion`)

**Version:** 0.1 DRAFT, 2026-10-21. English source text for recording; not approved (see the [consent index](README.md)).

**Kind:** `criterion`. **Where:** supervised sites only (never at open public kiosks), Stages 2, 3 and 5.
**Delivered by:** the trained field administrator reads the script aloud from the tablet (or plays the
approved recording), shows the pictograms, and answers questions. Then the participant taps on the
administrator device.
**Decision:** tap "yes" to join the questionnaire part, "no" to play the games only (core consent still
applies).
**Records:** consent artefact `kind: criterion`, administrator id, device id, language version.
**Duration of the questionnaire part:** [TBD: confirmed from the battery design, about 25 to 35 minutes]

## Script

### Segment 1 (`consent.criterion.purpose`)

> Thank you for coming. Today we are studying whether these games show the same things as questions that
> researchers already use. So we would like you to play the games, and also answer some questions with me,
> and do two small puzzles with cards and a wooden board. The questions are about how you usually feel and
> act in daily life. There are no right or wrong answers.

### Segment 2 (`consent.criterion.link`)

> To compare the games with your answers, we give you this card with a code. We scan it on the game machine
> and on my tablet, so the two records are joined. The code is not your name. Your name and phone number are
> never stored with your answers.

### Segment 3 (`consent.criterion.voluntary`)

> You can skip any question you do not want to answer. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and
> ask us to delete everything. Stopping will not affect [the payment for your time / any service you receive
> here]. **[IEC REVIEW: compensation wording]**

### Segment 4 (`consent.criterion.privacy`)

> Your answers are kept safely and used only for this research. Researchers see them only with the code, not
> your name. We keep them until [period] after the study ends and then delete them. You can ask a question or
> complain at the help desk, by phone at [number], or to the Data Protection Board of India.

### Comprehension check (administrator asks, participant answers in own words)

1. "If you do not want to answer a question, what can you do?" (skip it)
2. "Is your name kept with your answers?" (no)
3. "Can you stop?" (yes, any time)

If an answer is wrong, the administrator repeats the relevant segment once. If still unclear, the
participant plays the games only (core consent) and is thanked.

### Decision prompt (`consent.criterion.decision`)

> Would you like to answer the questions as well? Please touch the tick for yes, or the cross for no.

## Notes

- Audio-assisted administration of items for low-literacy participants uses approved item audio; licensed
  item wording is loaded from the licensed source and is not part of this script.
- The administrator never sees game scores (access control policy).
- **[IEC REVIEW]** compensation amount and form, and whether compensation is paid regardless of completion
  (recommended).

## ITC notes specific to this script

- "Questions researchers already use" should not suggest an exam or a government survey.
- The pid card wording ("code, not your name") is tested with low-literacy participants, who may associate
  cards with identity documents.
