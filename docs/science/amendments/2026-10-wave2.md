# Amendment 2026-10-wave2: dictionary phases, trial minimums and module budgets

Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: wave 2 integration (Pathway A episodes, Pathway B micro-games and hub). Inputs: the
wave 2 verifier reports (scored bot exports of every module), `docs/science/VAL-02-trial-requirements.md` and
`docs/science/indicator-review.md`. Every threshold stays in YAML/JSON (`stats/measurement-model/dictionary/*.yaml`,
`content/games/**`); this note records what changed and why. No dictionary minimum was lowered.

## A1. `stability.shift_slope_rude_run` (dictionary A): no change

The verifiers asked for `main` in its phases. The indicator review (PR #6) had already set phases
`[main, shift_post]` (calm baseline `tone_calm` is phase `main`, the rude run is `shift_post`); the merged
dictionary was checked and the Stall export scores it. Recorded here so the request is closed.

## A2. `expressive_restraint.iti_rvi` (A and B): include the pressure phase, restore the minimums

Problem: with phases `[main]` the indicator sat at the edge of its supply. The indicator review lowered the
minimums (A 30 to 20, B 40 to 30) to make it scorable; in A the selected block (Stall service, 20 planned,
18 protected) can still deliver fewer than 20 trials when the soft cap trims it.

Change (preferring more trials over lower minimums, as the review recommends):

| Pathway | Phases | Minimum | Planned / protected trials selected |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (Stall `stall.dont_serve`) | `[main, pressure]` | 30 (restored) | 38 / 34 |
| B (Don't-Tap `dont_tap.gng`) | `[main, pressure]` | 40 (restored) | 68 / 66 |

Justification: these are the same scored go/no-go trials the sibling indicators `premature_taps` and
`extra_taps` already use with `[main, pressure]`. To keep the timed pressure block's faster pace from
inflating the variability, the formula `style.rvi_inter_tap_interval` now forms intervals within a block only
and re-centres each block's detrended intervals on the pooled mean (with one block it is unchanged). The
indicator stays exploratory and not gate-eligible (`val02_pending`).

## A3. Phase reconciliation: Road Home errand maps and Weighing Post decisions (dictionary A)

The scorer flagged `phase_mismatch_fallback` because the dictionary selected `main` while the content blocks
are tagged with the shift phases. The content tags are the semantically right ones (they define the pre/post
cells of the shift slopes), so the dictionary side changed:

- Road Home errand maps (`errand_maps`, phase `shift_pre`; the pre-reversal baseline also used by
  `stability.shift_slope_reversal`): `task_organisation.errand_order_efficiency`, `.revisits`, `.idle_time`,
  `.resource_allocation_efficiency`, `problem_solving.excess_route_cost`, `initiative.route_planning_time`,
  `initiative.optional_actions_unprompted` now select `[shift_pre]`.
- Weighing Post weigh decisions (`decisions_pre` / `decisions_post`, phases `shift_pre` / `shift_post`, 7 + 7
  planned, 6 + 6 protected): `decisiveness.time_to_commit`, `.reversals_before_confirm`, `.info_gathered`,
  `.commit_rvi` now select `[shift_pre, shift_post]`. The minimum of 12 decisions needs both blocks.

`adaptability.post_reversal_errors` and `reversal_trials_to_reacquire` keep `[shift_post]` (post-reversal
trials only), which is correct. `response_consistency.loc_scale_sigma` (A) already selects every scored RT
phase (PR #6).

## A4. Wait-or-Now: `self_control.choice_consistency` and `persistence.effort_completion_rate` (dictionary B)

The 8-trial titrated effort staircase (indicator review section 4) was costed against the measured play:
8 delay + 8 effort has a slow estimate of 126.9 s, above the 120 s module limit; 8 effort + 6 delay needs a
118 s slot cap, which is +24 s on the session ceiling. After A5 the ceiling is fully allocated (890 s), so the
staircase is not adopted in v1. Both indicators stay `not_scorable_v1` (not gate-eligible) and their notes
now carry this budget rationale. Wait-or-Now now logs a chosen-but-never-pressed churn as an incomplete hold
(`input.hold`, `hold_ms` 0, `completed` false), so `effort_completion_rate` is unbiased when it is revisited.
The slot caps were aligned with the other slot modules (93 / 94 s), which costs nothing because the session
ceiling already counts the largest slot cap.

## A5. Pathway B budgets from measured play

| Module | Change | Reason (measured, bot exports) |
|---|---|---|
| Card Sort | intro 5.5 to 10 s; s/trial L1 2.7/3.2, L2 2.5/3.0, L3 2.3/2.8; planned 10/11/12 (minimums 8/10/10 unchanged); caps 99/100 to 114/119 s (the 5 s soft-to-hard gap covers one in-flight slow card plus the 1.5 s outro) | RT + ~0.95 s per card; slow adults were cut off inside the reversal block below its minimum |
| Don't-Tap | s/trial L1 1.27, L2 1.17, L3 1.01; caps 107/108 s | fixed pace measured at 1.163 s per trial (config 1.06); the soft cap trimmed pressure trials |
| Tower | caps 106/108 s | slow players lost the end-of-module probe block |

Card Sort's planned post-switch trials drop from 14 to 11-12 per switch (still above the 10 minimum and the
dictionary's 20-trial switch-cost minimum). VAL-02's +5 post-switch trials are not affordable at human pace.

To fund +35 s of hard caps within the 890 s target, the non-scored segment ceilings in
`content/games/session-plan.json` were reduced (the plan's section 4.4 values are upper bounds): onboarding
90 to 80 s, report + survey 90 to 75 s, contact opt-in 30 to 20 s (one yes/no with a 20 s timeout). Pathway B
ceilings: 531 s core + 94 s slot + 265 s segments = 890 s; play stays within its 660 s state ceiling.

## A6. Pathway A budgets

The segment reduction leaves Pathway A 25 s below the target, used for slow-player headroom:

- The Road Home: hard cap 196 to 200 s (soft cap 194 s); the soft-to-hard gap (6 s) now covers the 3 s
  outro.
- The Weighing Post: caps 194/196 to 200/204 s, so slow players reach the post-shift block and the probes.

Pathway A ceilings: 196 + 204 + 200 + 265 = 865 s. Medians are unchanged (all within 180-216 s).

## Not changed

- `content/games/pathway-a/stall.json` keeps its new `constraints` keys (`serve_items`, `dont_serve_reasons`,
  `probe_options`); the content tests accept them.
- No other dictionary minimum, gate or hypothesis changed. VAL-02 should be re-run with Stage 1 pilot data
  before SCR-03 freezes the trial budget, as the VAL-02 report recommends.
